Trust & Safety

HappyDonates is a connection platform. We do not process payments. Always verify the beneficiary directly before providing assistance.

Verification Checklist

1

Check IDs: Ask to see the Hospital ID, School ID, or Government ID related to the request.

2

Direct Payment: If money is requested, offer to pay the institution (School/Hospital) directly via their official bank account.

3

Meet Safely: For physical item donations, meet in public, well-lit areas.
